# v0.3 source review

The v0.2 model, derivative identities and dyadic annular comparison are
unchanged. This extension adds geometry, rate assembly and an exact positive
edge expansion.

- Inclusive separation uses strict annular interiors. When boundaries meet,
  a nearest-neighbor edge cannot touch both interiors. Boundary-only edges
  remain allocated to the ordinary Gibbs weight.
- The two-endpoint proof uses the complements of the cuts around the second
  endpoint. A global rotation and an inverse cut rotation give the required
  complementary-cut comparison. All cuts form one chain and every kernel
  edge set is disjoint from the other strict interiors.
- The two-endpoint count is based on half the separation, producing a square
  of the factorial envelope. Stirling's estimate gives coefficient 2/log 2.
- Local-limit declarations transfer the exact envelope through bounded
  continuous two-site marginals, retaining the finite-volume constants.
- For signed couplings the active-edge expansion subtracts min(b_e,0) from
  the edge exponent. This constant shift leaves the normalized Gibbs law
  unchanged and makes every activity nonnegative. Disconnected terms vanish
  by an actual measure-preserving tetrahedral rotation of a separating cut.
- The active-edge endpoint is a bound by normalized connected active-set mass.
  Connectivity is defined by absence of a separating cut. No independence,
  stochastic domination, exponential-decay assumption or mixing certificate
  is inserted into the formal proof.

The analytical addendum's log-square bound and FK exponential comparison are
distinct, unformalized extensions. Their full mathematical derivations and
independent review memos are supplied in analytical-addendum/. They are not
hidden hypotheses or axioms of any new Lean endpoint.

The machine records in latest/ cover the v0.3.0 source and dependency closure.
Version 0.3.1 changes only one scoped heartbeat option in mathematical source;
its default-elaboration maintenance checks are recorded in heartbeat-fix/.
Historical records in v0.2/ refer only to that earlier version. Reproduction
from a fresh checkout uses scripts/verify.py, which recompiles every module;
the release-specific extension check also freshly compiles the full standalone
source and replays its resulting object.
